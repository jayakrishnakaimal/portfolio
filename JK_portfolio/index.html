01Philosophy

What I believe about
design leadership

"

Great design is not decoration — it is the clearest expression of strategy. My job is to make that visible to everyone in the room.

01
Clarity Over Cleverness
Every decision must reduce friction, not add beauty for its own sake. If a user has to think about it, we haven't finished designing it.
02
Systems Before Screens
I build reusable thinking, not one-off solutions. A component delivered once is debt. A system delivered once is leverage.
03
Influence Through Evidence
I equip teams with research, data, and narrative to earn a seat at every table. Opinion is a starting point; evidence is the close.
04
People Are the Product
The best output of a great design manager is not a great interface — it's a great team. I invest more in growing designers than reviewing their work.
02Case Studies

Selected work

End-to-end stories — problem framing, team leadership, process decisions, and measurable outcomes.

The Problem

68% of new enterprise accounts dropped off within 7 days. Customer Success called it a training problem. I reframed it as a design problem — specifically, a missing mental model.

Ran 18 moderated sessions across 3 enterprise tiers, mapped a 47-step journey users expected in 8, analysed 6 months of Mixpanel funnels, and shadowed 4 CSM calls to surface the unspoken pain.

How I Led the Team

Wk 1–2 · Align — Cross-functional kickoff. One metric: time-to-first-value 7d → 2d.

Wk 3–4 · Diverge — 2-day sprint. 60+ concepts → 3 directions via weighted matrix.

Wk 5–8 · Test — 3 usability rounds. 48-hr cycles. Introduced "80% reviews" to unblock perfectionism.

Wk 9–12 · Ship — Phased delivery. Component specs. QA. 15% cohort → full rollout.

The Problem

4,200+ inconsistent UI elements. Three teams building independently. Engineers recreating UI every sprint. Design reviews 3× slower than needed.

Leadership saw visual inconsistency. I reframed it as a velocity and trust problem — customers were losing confidence in the product's coherence.

Strategy & Execution

Design System Council — 1 designer per squad + 1 eng lead. Shared ownership from day one.

Full audit — 4,200 → 180 core components. "Pave the cowpath" — standardised what teams did well, not what I wished they did.

Semantic tokens as npm package, consumed by all 3 repos. Monthly Office Hours drove adoption faster than any mandate.

04Leadership

How I build &
grow teams

My leadership impact is measured by the growth of the people around me — not the quality of my own deliverables.

🎯
Hiring Philosophy
I hire for curiosity and craft, then invest in everything else. I look for designers who ask "why" before "what" — and who treat feedback as a gift, not a threat. I have never made a hire I regretted by rushing the process.

Every candidate goes through a structured process: portfolio review, design challenge, culture interview, and a reference check with someone they didn't list. The thoroughness shows the candidate how seriously I take building a team they'll want to stay on.
