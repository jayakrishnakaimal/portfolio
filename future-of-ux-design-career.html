How AI Changes Your Work
Area What Changes Disruption
Wireframing & Prototyping AI generates low-fi → hi-fi in seconds. Your job shifts to critique, direction & validation. High
Research Synthesis AI summarises transcripts & tags themes. Designers own insight interpretation & ethics. Medium
Conversational / AI UX New specialisation: designing prompts, error states, tone & guardrails for AI products. Opportunity
Design Systems AI enforces tokens & component rules automatically. DS becomes strategic, not maintenance. Medium
Accessibility Audits Automated contrast, ARIA, and flow checks. Human judgment remains for context & edge cases. Augmented
User Testing AI simulates user flows & generates test scripts. Live moderated testing becomes premium. Medium

Key Takeaways
  • 1
    Designers who use AI tools will replace designers who don't — embrace it as a collaborator, not a threat.
  • 2
    Systems thinking and strategic vision overtake pixel craft as the most valued senior skill.
  • 3
    AI/Conversational UX and Spatial Design are the fastest-growing specialisations through 2030.
  • 4
    AI literacy is already commanding 20–30% salary premiums in product design roles.
  • 5
    UX Research remains resilient and high-value — human empathy and ethical judgment cannot be automated.
  • 6
    Design-to-code fluency (React, tokens, design systems) separates top earners from the rest.
