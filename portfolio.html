Design process

How I work.

From discovery to delivery, a repeatable way of working that keeps users, product and engineering aligned.

01
Phase 01

Understand

Know the problem before drawing a screen.

Discovery

Frame the problem first: business goals, user needs and constraints.

User Research

Interviews, surveys and field observation with users and operators, turned into clear insights.

Competitive Analysis

Compare rival products to find gaps, strong patterns and room to stand out.

Heuristic Evaluation

Review existing screens against usability principles to spot issues quickly and cheaply.

Personas & Journey Maps

Show who the users are and where their journey breaks down.

02
Phase 02

Shape

Turn insight into ideas, then into something real.

IBM Design Thinking

Enterprise Design Thinking, with Hills, user loops and cross‑discipline teams, keeps work on outcomes.

Iterate

Sketch, test and refine in short cycles that move closer to real needs.

Prototype

Clickable, interactive prototypes test ideas early and show stakeholders the real experience.

Concept Testing

Test early concepts with internal users to get quick, honest feedback before investing in build.

03
Phase 03

Deliver

Ship consistent work and keep everyone in step.

Figma & Design Systems

Shared components and style guides in Figma help teams ship consistent work faster. Every design is signed off by me, and nothing goes to dev without my sign‑off.

Playbacks

Regular playbacks and peer reviews show progress, gather feedback and keep sponsors aligned.

Design QA

Designs handed to dev are QA'd every time, so the live code stays aligned with the design shared.

IBM Enterprise Design Thinking

The IBM Loop: observe, reflect, make

Every phase runs on the Loop. I observe what users do and need, reflect to align the team on what it means, then make something to test. Each pass sharpens the next, so the work keeps closing in on real outcomes instead of assumptions.

ObserveReflectMakeRepeat continuously
Runs through every phase

I work as a trio with Product Managers and Development or Tech leads, sharing ownership from discovery to delivery. Alongside that, I set expectations early, communicate clearly and bring leaders along on decisions and trade‑offs.
